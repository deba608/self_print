Start here

Scan the QR code at the counter

Point your phone camera at the QR code posted at the shop. It opens the SelfPrint upload page directly in your browser — no app to install, no account required to get started.

Use your own mobile data — not the shop WiFi. Your files upload directly from your phone, so a personal connection is faster and more secure.
Step 2

Upload your file

Tap the upload box on the page and select your file. You can add multiple files to one order.

→

Supported formats

PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX

→

DOC / DOCX files

Converted to PDF automatically before printing. This takes about a minute — keep the tab open while it runs.

→

Multiple files

You can upload more than one file in a single order. Each file gets its own print settings.

Step 3

Choose print settings

Configure how each file should be printed. Your last-used settings are remembered on this device for next time.

Print type — B&W or Color
Copies — how many you need
Paper size — A4, A3, Photo…
Duplex — single or double-sided
Pages per sheet — fit multiple pages
Page range — all pages or select

Add-ons (if offered): spiral binding, cover page, bond paper.

Step 4

Add a note (optional)

Use the note box to leave any special instructions for the shop — for example: "staple pages", "urgent", or "print only from page 3 onwards".

Maximum 250 characters.

Step 5

Choose pickup or home delivery

🏪 Pickup at counter
Collect your prints at the shop. Pay by cash, UPI, or card when you arrive.
🚚 Home delivery
Available for PDF and image orders in serviceable pincodes. Online payment required upfront (UPI / Razorpay). Enter your address, pincode, and confirm location.
DOC / DOCX orders cannot use home delivery — choose pickup for those. Delivery also requires online payment; cash-at-door is not available.
Step 6

Submit and get your order token

After you submit, you'll receive an order token. This is your reference number for the entire order.

🎫
Your order token
Write it down or keep the page open. You'll need it to track your order and collect your prints at the counter.
  • Paid online? Your order starts processing right away.
  • Paying at the counter? Show your token to staff when you arrive.
Step 7

Track your order

Go to /track and enter your token to see live status:

Uploaded › Approved › Printing › Ready / Delivered

If you registered an account, /my-jobs shows all your past and current orders — no token needed.

Step 8

Payment

Pay at counter
Cash, UPI, or card. Staff mark it paid once received. Works for all pickup orders.
Pay online
UPI or Razorpay checkout during submission. Required for all home delivery orders.
Step 9

Collect or receive your order

→

Pickup

Come to the counter once your order status shows Ready. Show your token to staff.

→

Home delivery

Your order is dispatched to the address you entered. Track the status at /track the same way.

That's it. Your prints are ready. No account required, no app to uninstall — just scan again next time.
Good to know

Tips before you submit

  • Keep the browser tab open until you see your token — closing early may lose your order.
  • Large files take longer to upload. A stable mobile data connection helps.
  • Check the page range and orientation in the preview before submitting — once staff approve the job, printing starts immediately.
