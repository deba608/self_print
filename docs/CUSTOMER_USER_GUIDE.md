# SelfPrint — Customer User Guide

A simple guide to printing your documents using SelfPrint. No app install needed — just scan and go.

## 1. Scan the QR code

Scan the QR code posted at the shop counter with your phone camera. It opens the SelfPrint upload page in your browser. Use your own mobile data (not shop WiFi) so your files upload directly from your phone.

## 2. Upload your file

- Tap the upload box and pick your file, or drag-and-drop on desktop.
- Supported: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX.
- You can upload more than one file in one order.
- DOC/DOCX files are converted to PDF automatically before printing — this may take a minute.

## 3. Choose print settings

For each file, set:

- **Print type** — Black & White or Color
- **Copies** — how many copies you need
- **Paper size** — A4, A3, Photo, etc.
- **Duplex** — single-sided or double-sided (only shown when the file supports it)
- **Layout / pages per sheet** — fit multiple pages on one sheet if needed
- **Page range** — print all pages or select specific ones
- **Add-ons** (if offered) — spiral binding, cover page, bond paper

Your last-used settings are remembered on this device for next time.

## 4. Add a note (optional)

Use the note box to tell the shop staff anything special about your order (e.g. "staple pages", "urgent"). Max 250 characters.

## 5. Choose pickup or home delivery

- **Pickup** — collect your prints at the counter, pay there (cash/UPI/card).
- **Home delivery** — available for image/PDF orders in serviceable pincodes. Delivery requires online payment upfront (via UPI/Razorpay), since the shop can't collect cash at your door. Enter your address, pincode, and confirm your location when prompted.

DOC/DOCX orders can't use home delivery (they need conversion first) — pickup only.

## 6. Submit and get your token

After submitting, you'll receive an **order token**. This is your reference number — write it down or keep the page open.

- If you paid online, your order starts processing right away.
- If you chose pickup with pay-at-counter, show your token to staff.

## 7. Track your order

- Go to `/track` and enter your token to check live status (uploaded → approved → printing → ready/delivered).
- If you registered/logged in, `/my-jobs` shows all your past and current orders without needing the token.

## 8. Payment

- **Pay at counter**: cash, UPI, or card — staff will mark it paid once received.
- **Pay online**: UPI or Razorpay checkout during submission (required for home delivery).

## 9. Collect or receive your order

- **Pickup**: show your token at the counter once status shows "ready".
- **Delivery**: your order is dispatched to the address you provided; track status the same way.

## Tips

- Keep the browser tab open until you see your token — closing early may lose your order.
- Large files take longer to upload; a stable connection helps.
- If a page looks wrong in preview, re-check your page range/orientation before submitting — once approved, staff already start printing.
