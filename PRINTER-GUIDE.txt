==================================================
   SELFPRINT - SHOP PRINTER GUIDE (easy version)
==================================================

This computer runs the PRINTER SERVICE for your shop.
Customers upload files from their phone. You take payment
and release the print. This computer prints it.

You do NOT need to know any coding. Just follow below.

--------------------------------------------------
 EVERY DAY - WHAT YOU DO
--------------------------------------------------

NOTHING. Just turn the computer ON.

The printer service starts BY ITSELF. A small green
window opens on its own and says "Connected".
That means printing is LIVE.

  -> Leave that green window alone. Do not close it.
  -> If you accidentally close it, just double-click
     the green icon named START-PRINTER to open again.

That is all. No clicking, no setup, every day.

--------------------------------------------------
 HOW A PRINT JOB WORKS
--------------------------------------------------

1. Customer scans your QR code, uploads file, gets a TOKEN number.
2. On your ADMIN screen (the website), find that token.
3. Customer pays at counter -> you click  PAID.
4. You click  RELEASE PRINT.
5. The printer prints in a few seconds. Done.

--------------------------------------------------
 IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------

PROBLEM: Nothing prints after I click Release.
  - Is the green printer window open? If not, double-click START-PRINTER.
  - Is the printer turned on, has paper, no error light?
  - Does the green window say "Connected"? If it says red text, see below.

PROBLEM: The green window shows RED text / closed by itself.
  - Read the red message.
  - Take a PHOTO of the whole window with your phone.
  - Send it to the developer (see contact below).

PROBLEM: Printer prints to a file / asks to save instead of paper.
  - Wrong printer is selected.
  - On the ADMIN website, open Settings/Printer and pick your real
    printer (the actual machine), NOT "Microsoft Print to PDF".

PROBLEM: "Node.js is not installed" message.
  - Go to  https://nodejs.org , click the big LTS button, install it.
  - Then double-click START-PRINTER again.

PROBLEM: Internet is down.
  - Printing needs internet. Restore wifi/connection, then it works again.

--------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO REPORT A PROBLEM (so it gets fixed fast)
--------------------------------------------------

Send the developer ALL of these:
  1. What you were doing (e.g. "clicked Release Print, nothing happened").
  2. The customer TOKEN number, if any.
  3. A PHOTO of the green printer window (especially any red text).
  4. Is the printer on, with paper? Yes / No.
  5. Is internet working on this PC? Yes / No.
